# UCI Advanced Placement Credit Mapping — 2026–27

Research date: July 24, 2026.

Official source: [UCI AP and IB Credit](https://catalogue.uci.edu/informationforprospectivestudents/undergraduateadmissions/#advancedplacementandinternationalbaccalaureatecredittext)

This is the human-readable seed reference for AP onboarding and audit tests. The production importer should still retain a dated copy of the official table and its footnotes.

## Global rules

- Scores of 3, 4, or 5 receive the award shown below; a blank unit value means no university units.
- AP, IB, and college work may duplicate one another. UCI awards credit only once.
- A student cannot earn UCI units or grade points in a course from which AP/IB credit already exempts them. If taken anyway, the UCI course is specially coded without unit or grade credit.
- Some exams exempt more UCI course units than the AP units awarded. In that case, the student may take the final course in the sequence for credit.
- Exam credit has no letter grade and must not be included in GPA calculations.
- Awarded units count toward graduation but do not force earlier major declaration or graduation.

### CS + Mathematics consequences

- AP Computer Science A does **not** clear I&C SCI 31, 32, or 33.
- AP Computer Science Principles awards I&C SCI 20, which is not the CS major programming series.
- AP Statistics does **not** clear the CS requirement STATS 67.
- AP Physics does not award PHYSICS 7C, 7D, or 7E.
- AP Calculus BC 4–5 is the most direct AP reduction to both the CS and Mathematics lower-division plans.
- A Calculus BC score below 3 can still produce the Calculus AB award when the AB subscore is 3 or higher.

## Cross-exam caps and implementation notes

- Maximum credit across the two AP English exams is eight units.
- The Art and Design family carries a combined maximum of eight units.
- The Physics exam family carries a combined maximum of eight units.
- The two official Statistics equivalence rows carry a combined maximum of four units.
- Preserve program-specific exclusions attached to Art History, Economics, Government, History, and English awards; a course equivalence is not always permission to use that course in every major.
- Store the official exam name, score band, units, equivalence alternatives, GE/UC effect, cap group, and source version separately. Do not reduce the table to a single `equivalentCourse` string.
- When multiple awards point to the same course or requirement, the audit should explain the duplicate and count the effect only once.

## Required seed tests

1. Calculus AB 3 awards four units and a MATH 2A/MATH 5A alternative.
2. Calculus BC 3 awards eight units but only the first course in the applicable calculus sequence.
3. Calculus BC 4–5 awards eight units and the first two courses in the applicable calculus sequence.
4. The BC AB-subscore rule works even when the overall BC score is below 3.
5. Computer Science A never satisfies an ICS programming prerequisite.
6. Computer Science Principles maps to I&C SCI 20.
7. Statistics 3–5 may map to STATS 7 but never STATS 67.
8. Chemistry 4–5 maps to CHEM 1A or ENGR 1A plus elective units.
9. Both English exams do not award 16 units.
10. Research/Seminar may satisfy ELWR while awarding zero units.
11. Duplicate AP and UCI course credit produces no second unit or grade award.
12. Language placement requirements remain visible after an AP award.
