# The University of Texas at Austin — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## University-wide
- External transfer = at least **24 transferable credit hours** complete or in progress at the time
  of application. Transcripts of ALL college AND high school coursework required.
- General summer/fall applications are due **March 1** with supplemental materials due March 10;
  spring applications are due **September 1** with supplemental materials due September 8.
- Entry terms differ by admitting college and major: Cockrell ECE is **fall-only**; Computer Science
  is **fall-only**; Mathematics accepts **summer, fall, and spring** transfers.
- UT publishes an **Automated Transfer Equivalency (ATE)** database, but it covers Texas
  institutions; out-of-state coursework is evaluated during admission review.

Sources:
- https://admissions.utexas.edu/apply/transfer-students/
- https://admissions.utexas.edu/explore/colleges-degrees/

## Cockrell School of Engineering — Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)
Source: https://cockrell.utexas.edu/admissions/undergraduate/external-transfer/
- Fall admission only; application due **March 1**.
- Students are admitted to a **specific major**; apply to the major you intend to finish in.
- Required equivalents for every Cockrell transfer applicant:
  - M 408D **or** M 408L **or** M 408M (second-semester calculus / sequences; TCCN MATH 2415)
  - PHY 303K engineering physics I (TCCN PHYS 2425)
  - PHY 103M physics lab
- Plus **four technical courses** total (math, physics, chemistry, biology, geology, computer
  science, engineering) — the three above may count toward the four.
- Performance in technical coursework (calculus, chemistry, physics) is carefully reviewed.
- Only courses in the engineering degree plan (or chair-approved equivalents) count toward the degree;
  a course may transfer for credit yet not apply to the degree.
- **ECE-specific cap: at most 25 semester credit hours of electrical-engineering transfer courses
  may count toward the ECE degree.**
- Expect a minimum of five long semesters in residence; most transfers take six to seven.
- If you enroll at UT in a different major intending to switch in later, you need at least one year
  in residence at UT and must win a competitive internal transfer.
- UT Austin has **no standalone Computer Engineering B.S.** — computer engineering is inside the
  **Electrical and Computer Engineering** degree. The nearest separate degree is the
  **B.S. in Computational Engineering** (Cockrell, ASE/EM department, 122 SCH).

## College of Natural Sciences — Computer Science and Mathematics
Source: https://cns.utexas.edu/info-future-students/external-transfer-applications
- The College of Natural Sciences accepts transfers for summer, fall, and spring, but UT's current
  degree page explicitly limits Computer Science transfer admission to fall. Mathematics has no
  such exception and therefore retains all three CNS entry terms.
- At least 24 hours of transferable credit; select a Natural Sciences major on the Common App.
- CNS states no hard prerequisite list, but to be competitive an applicant should have:
  - demonstrated interest and ability in the field
  - a strong overall GPA
  - **credit for Calculus**
  - **at least six additional credit hours of first-year science courses** (for science majors)
  - **a minimum grade of B- in ALL math and science courses**
- Computer Science transfer seats are very limited; most openings go to UT internal transfers.
- Mathematics B.S. supports pathways in pure mathematics, **applied mathematics**, statistics and
  probability, data science, scientific computation, actuarial science, and UTeach — so the user's
  "UT Austin Math" and "UT Austin Applied Math" are ONE admission target (Mathematics) with two
  in-major pathways.
