# Georgia Institute of Technology — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31. Course chart read directly from the live DOM (icons, not text).

## General transfer requirements
Source: https://admission.gatech.edu/transfer/application-requirements
- Minimum 30 semester (45 quarter) credit hours completed.
- Minimum GPA: 3.3 for College of Computing and College of Engineering;
  3.0 for College of Sciences, College of Design, Ivan Allen, Scheller.
- Two English composition courses (6 semester hours) equivalent to ENGL 1101 + ENGL 1102.
- Must be a high school graduate before the application period.
- Official transfer credit evaluation happens only AFTER an offer of admission.

## Course requirements by major
Source: https://admission.gatech.edu/transfer/course-requirements-major
- ALL MAJORS require: ENGL 1101, ENGL 1102, MATH 1551 (Calculus I).
- All major course requirements must transfer with a grade of C or better.
- Lab science electives: biology, chemistry, calculus-based physics, earth & atmospheric, or
  environmental science; must include lecture AND lab; >= 4 semester or 6 quarter credit hours.

Notes:
- Georgia Tech has NO separate "Applied Mathematics" degree. The user's "GT Applied Math" maps to
  the **Mathematics B.S.** (School of Mathematics) — applied work is a concentration/thread inside
  it — or to **Mathematics & Computing B.S.** Both rows are given above.
- Computer Science is in the College of Computing (not Engineering) and is excluded from the
  Regents' Engineering Pathway Program; CS transfers apply through General Transfer.
- ECE (CmpE and EE) transfer admission is extremely competitive; GT warns that finishing the
  degree may take as many as seven more semesters.

## Entry terms and deadlines
Source: https://admission.gatech.edu/transfer/deadlines-fees

All five requested programs accept general-transfer applications for fall, spring, and summer.
Architecture is the only major the page singles out as fall-only.

| Entry term | Application window | Documents | Course requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall | January 18–March 1 | March 16 | May 30 |
| Spring | August 1–September 15 | October 1 | Mid-September |
| Summer | December 1–January 15 | February 1 | May 15 |

The course-requirement deadline is distinct from the application and document deadlines, so the
planner displays all three rather than reducing each cycle to one date.
