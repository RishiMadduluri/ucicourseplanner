# Harvard College — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.
Source: https://college.harvard.edu/admissions/apply/transfer-applicants

## Scale
> "Each fall, we welcome a very small number (**12 on average**) of transfer students to Harvard College."
> "Recently, we have received **more than 2,000 transfer applications per year**."

## Eligibility
- Must have completed **at least one continuous academic year (fall and spring) in a full-time
  degree program at ONE college, and not more than two academic years**, by the time of enrollment.
- **"Students are not permitted to relinquish credit earned in order to restart their college
  experience or regain eligibility."**
- Ineligible if the prior coursework is part-time, online, night-time, extension/continuing
  education, performance-based, professional (business/finance/medical), or technical/vocational.
- No one holding a bachelor's degree from any university, U.S. or foreign, may be admitted.
- **Fall entry only.** Deadline **March 1** for the application and all financial aid materials;
  decisions by June. College transcripts should be sent between January and March.

## What Harvard looks for
- A clearly defined academic need to transfer
- A proven record of achievement at the current institution
- Strong faculty recommendations
- "Achievement in a rigorous program of study, **especially in your expected field of specialization**."

## Application requirements
- Common Application Transfer Application + Harvard writing supplement, $95 fee
- **SAT or ACT is required of all transfer applicants** (AP/IB/A-level/national exam results may
  substitute only in exceptional cases where SAT/ACT is inaccessible)
- College/Dean's/Registrar's report, official college transcript,
  **two college instructor recommendations**, official high school transcript

## Transfer credit
- **Maximum 16 semester-long courses** (two full years) may transfer; the remaining half of the
  A.B./S.B. must be taken at Harvard.
- Eligible: courses in a **full-time, daytime** program at an accredited degree-granting institution,
  comparable to Harvard's liberal arts curriculum, with **grades of C or better**.
- **Not eligible:** online or distance courses; night or extension courses; part-time coursework;
  technical/vocational courses (accounting, marketing, journalism, law, agriculture, nursing,
  business, communications, specialized military training); dual-enrollment credit counted toward a
  high school diploma; work done in an isolated term at another college unless your current college
  granted credit for it; credit gained by accelerating; **CLEP, AP, or IB — any credit earned by
  examination, even when your current college credited it**; summer school courses except those at
  your current college or Harvard Summer School.

## Majors requested (concentrations — no separate admission)
- **Applied Mathematics, A.B./S.B.** (SEAS)
- **Computer Science, A.B./S.B.** (SEAS)
- **Mathematics, A.B.** (FAS)
Harvard admits to the College; concentrations are declared in the sophomore year. There are no
published per-concentration transfer prerequisites.
