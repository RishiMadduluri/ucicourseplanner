## Eligibility
- Must have completed at least **one term as a full-time student in a bachelor's-degree-seeking
  program** elsewhere before matriculating.
- Should have at least one year and no more than two years of college credit by enrollment.
- No published minimum GPA; successful applicants are typically around 3.5+ in rigorous coursework.
- Application requires the UChicago Supplement (extended essay + short "why UChicago") plus a
  **short essay explaining why you are leaving your current college**, an official high school
  transcript, and official transcripts from every post-secondary school attended.

## Transfer-credit rules (these directly constrain how UCI work counts)
A course is eligible only if it:
- was taken at an accredited bachelor's-degree-granting institution;
- is sufficiently similar to a UChicago College offering in subject, content, and learning outcomes;
- confers **at least 3 semester hours or 4 quarter hours** (or 30 contact hours);
- was completed with a **quality letter grade of C or above — not C-, not P**;
- does not duplicate credit already earned by exam, other transfer credit, or UChicago enrollment.
Ineligible categories include:
- courses taken **before the high school diploma/GED**;
- courses taken elsewhere after matriculating, **or during the summer immediately before matriculating**;
- courses taken through a **primarily online program or institution**;
- courses not considered college level at UChicago (e.g. pre-calculus);
- professional/technical/vocational courses.
Syllabi (with subject code, instructor, description, learning outcomes, meeting schedule, texts,
assignments, and grading) may be required for any course without a recent approval history.

## Limits, residency, and pacing
- **Max 1200 units of transfer credit per academic year** at a previous institution and
  **max 400 units per individual term** (including summer).
- Semester conversion: two semester-length courses ≈ three UChicago quarter courses.
  (A UCI quarter course maps roughly one-to-one onto a UChicago quarter course.)
- **Residency: at least six quarters in residence (excluding summers) and a minimum of 1800 units
  — eighteen 100-unit courses — completed by enrollment in UChicago courses.**
- **At least half of the requirements for any major or minor must be completed in residence.**
- Transfer students get a prorated number of quarters of enrollment (minimum six) based on credit awarded.
- From the summer immediately before enrolling, a transfer student **may not earn additional credit
  from any other college**.
- An enrollment hold is applied in Autumn Quarter if final official transcripts are not submitted.

## Majors requested
All are College majors with no separate departmental admission — admission is to the College.
- **Computational and Applied Mathematics (CAAM), B.S./B.A.** — the user's "Computational and Applied Math"
- **Computer Science, B.A./B.S.**
- **Data Science, B.A./B.S.**
- **Mathematics, B.A./B.S.**
Departmental credit toward a specific major requirement is granted either during the transfer-credit
evaluation under the department's Catalog terms, or afterwards via a General Petition to the
department contact.
