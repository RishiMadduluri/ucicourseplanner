# Northwestern University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.
Source: https://admissions.northwestern.edu/faqs/transferring-to-northwestern/

Northwestern is on the **quarter system**, like UCI, and states its expectations in quarter hours.

## Eligibility and process
- Anyone who has enrolled in and earned credits from post-secondary study after secondary school
  must apply as a transfer.
- **"Successful transfer applicants typically arrive at Northwestern having completed at least one
  academic year of full-time college coursework (24 semester hours or 36 QUARTER hours)."**
- Must be in good standing and eligible to continue at the most recent institution.
- **Fall-quarter enrollment only.**
- Deadlines: **Transfer Early Decision March 1**, **Regular Decision April 1**.
- **Test-optional** for transfer candidates (SAT/ACT not required).
- Required: Common Application or Coalition/Scoir with Transfer Writing Supplements; official
  secondary school transcript proving graduation; all official university transcripts; College
  Report; **one academic evaluation (letter of recommendation)**.
- **Transfer applicants may apply to all departments** at Northwestern's undergraduate schools
  (Theatre in the School of Communication has very limited space; Bienen requires an audition).
- Admitted transfers must live on campus for their first year unless entering as a third/fourth-year
  student or 21+ by September 1.

## Credit rules
- Registrar does the official evaluation; a preliminary evaluation follows an offer of admission.
- **Undergraduate Registration Requirement:** an incoming transfer pursuing a four-year degree
  generally needs **6 quarters (two full academic years) of full-time study at Northwestern**.
  This minimum also caps how much AP/transfer/external credit can apply to the degree.
- **These do NOT transfer:** courses from unaccredited institutions; physical education or hygiene;
  credit granted by institutional or CLEP examination rather than attendance; **courses worth less
  than three quarter hours or two semester hours**; ESL courses; **courses graded P (Pass) or
  S (Satisfactory)**; work in curricular areas Northwestern does not recognize for credit.

### Test-credit correction

Source: https://catalogs.northwestern.edu/undergraduate/requirements-policies/transfer-credit/test-credit/

- Northwestern's undergraduate catalogue permits credit for eligible **AP** scores and
  **higher-level IB** scores. The credit and placement awarded depend on the exam, score, school,
  and degree program.
- This corrects the earlier project handoff that grouped Northwestern with Yale, Harvard, and
  Princeton as awarding no test credit. Northwestern's ban on institutional/CLEP examination credit
  and on P/S *coursework* is not a blanket ban on AP/IB test credit.

## Majors requested
- **Applied Mathematics** — McCormick School of Engineering, Department of Engineering Sciences and
  Applied Mathematics (ESAM), B.S.
- **Computer Science** — offered as a **B.S. in McCormick** and a **B.A. in Weinberg College of Arts
  and Sciences**. Transfer applicants apply to a school and may apply to any department.
