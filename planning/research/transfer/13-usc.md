# University of Southern California — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## University-wide (Office of Admission)
Source: https://admission.usc.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/transfer-students/
- **USC accepts BOTH sophomore and junior transfers** — "you can transfer to USC after just one year
  of study elsewhere." This is one of the few targets on the user's list open after a single year.
- Fall entry only. Application deadline **February 15** (Dec 1 for certain arts BFA programs).
  Decision or request-for-spring-grades by end of May; if spring grades were requested, decision mid-July.
- **No minimum number of transfer units.** With fewer than 30 transferable semester units by the end
  of spring, USC weighs the high school record (and SAT/ACT if submitted) heavily.
- Successful applicants present at least one year of rigorous full-time coursework, typically
  **14–16 units per term**, with strong grades.
- Must submit official transcripts from ALL colleges attended, including pre-high-school-graduation work.
- Transfer applicants indicate a first-choice major, optionally a second choice or Undecided.
- Writing courses taken Pass/No Pass do NOT satisfy USC's lower-division writing requirement.
- Remedial coursework is not transferable.

## Viterbi School of Engineering (Computer Science, ECE, Computer Engineering and Computer Science)
Source: https://viterbiadmission.usc.edu/transfer/
- **No minimum GPA to apply**; competitive candidates show success in math, science/programming and
  engineering courses.
- Required courses MAY be in progress at application but must be finished by the end of the spring
  term before transferring.
- **Math requirement:** two math courses (calculus or beyond) after high school, with at least
  **Calculus II in progress at the point of application**. Major-specific math must be letter-graded.
  **AP/IB credit alone does NOT satisfy this.** Continued rigor expected — if Calc II is done, move
  to Calc III and beyond.
- **Science requirement:** two lab-based science courses (chemistry, or calculus-based physics) OR
  programming courses. Major-specific lab sciences must be **letter-graded and in person**.
  AP/IB alone does not satisfy this.
  - Computer science applicants may substitute programming courses for lab sciences. Note: USC says
    programming courses "very rarely transfer for course-equivalent credit" — they are preparation
    for USC placement exams, which can waive a course requirement.
  - **Electrical Engineering applicants should take physics.**
- **Writing requirement:** the equivalent of WRIT 130/150 — typically the second English composition
  course at your college.
- **Unit requirement: 30–32 transferable semester units per academic year (45–48 quarter units).**
  Fewer than 30 units => USC may request the high school transcript and spring grades.
- Recommended term shape: one math, one science/programming, one writing, one GE course each term.

### Computer Science, B.S. — lower-division target courses
Source: https://www.cs.usc.edu/academic-programs/undergrad/computer-science/
- CSCI 102L, CSCI 103L, CSCI 104L, CSCI 170, CSCI 201L —
  **C (2.0) or better required in each core CS course; a C- or below must be retaken, and may only
  be retaken once.**
- MATH 125g Calculus I, MATH 126g/129 Calculus II, MATH 226g/229 Calculus III,
  MATH 225 or MATH 235 Linear Algebra
- EE 364 Probability and Statistics
- One life science course, one physical science course (chemistry or physics)
- WRIT 150

### Computer Engineering and Computer Science, B.S.
Jointly administered by the Thomas Lord Department of Computer Science and the Ming Hsieh
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. Same Viterbi transfer requirements; adds the
EE lower-division core (EE 109, EE 209 etc.) on top of the CS core.

### Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), B.S.
Ming Hsieh Department. Viterbi transfer requirements apply; **physics is expected**, and lower
division targets are MATH 125, 126, 226, 245 and CHEM 105a (chemistry required for EE).

## Dornsife College — Applied and Computational Mathematics
Source: https://dornsife.usc.edu/mathematics/degree-requirements-undergrad/
- USC offers **Applied and Computational Mathematics B.S. and B.A.** with the SAME pre-major course
  requirements. Both require Probability Theory and Numerical Methods; the B.S. adds Mathematical
  Statistics, Fundamental Concepts of Analysis, and a logical-reasoning elective.
- Pre-major sequence: MATH 125, MATH 126, MATH 226, MATH 225 (or 235), plus a programming course.
- Admission is granted by the USC Office of Admission (no separate departmental application).
- Transfer students must complete all upper-division units of the major at USC, plus any remaining
  lower-division courses, WRIT 340, and at least two Core Literacy GE courses.
