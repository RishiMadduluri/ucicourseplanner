# UC San Diego — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## Campus-wide transfer rules
Source: https://admissions.ucsd.edu/transfer/application-requirements.html
- JUNIOR LEVEL ONLY. Minimum 60 UC-transferable semester (90 quarter) units by end of spring prior to fall.
- UCSD grants credit for at most 70 semester (105 quarter) units of LOWER-DIVISION coursework.
- Senior-standing risk: 70+ semester units lower-division AND 20+ semester units upper-division
  "could jeopardize admission."
- Minimum GPA 2.4 CA resident / 2.8 non-resident; admitted students are typically far above.
- 7-course pattern required — but NOT for students transferring from another UC campus.
- All courses completed at a UC campus (regular or summer, not Extension) are transferable.

## Selective (capped) majors — critical rule
Source: https://cse.ucsd.edu/undergraduate/cse-selective-major-process ,
        https://datascience.ucsd.edu/prospective-students/prospective-transfer-students/
- CSE and Data Science majors are SELECTIVE/CAPPED.
- Transfer students may NOT apply to the campus selective-major process after enrolling.
  You must be admitted DIRECTLY into the major at the point of admission. "There are no exceptions."
- ALL transfer major-preparation coursework must be COMPLETE to be eligible.
- Screening is on: number of prep courses completed, grades in them, and cumulative GPA.
- All screening courses need C (2.0) or better; 3.0+ in prep coursework recommended.
- Applicants are strongly encouraged to list an uncapped alternate major.
