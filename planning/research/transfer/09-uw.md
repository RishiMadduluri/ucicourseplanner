# University of Washington (Seattle) — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## Paul G. Allen School of CSE — Computer Science and Computer Engineering
Source: https://www.cs.washington.edu/academics/undergraduate/admissions/transfers/
Two-step process: (1) UW transfer application, (2) separate Allen School departmental application.
Missing the UW deadline makes you ineligible for the Allen School application.

Deadlines:
- Autumn entry: UW application Dec 15 – Feb 15; Allen School departmental application closes
  **April 5, 5:00 pm PT**; decisions early May.
- Spring entry: UW application Nov 1 – Dec 15; departmental application closes Jan 15; decisions
  early February. Spring transfer applicants can only be admitted to UW if admitted to the major.

Minimum academic requirements — all complete and numerically graded (>= 2.0) BEFORE the
departmental deadline; AP/IB credit is transferable but is NOT graded credit:
- **At least 30 graded credits**
- Math: MATH 124, MATH 125, MATH 126
- CS: CSE 143 (or CSE 123)
- English: five credits of English composition
- Natural science: PHYS 121 if applying to Computer Engineering; for Computer Science one of
  PHYS 121, BIOL 180, CHEM 142 (or a course from the CS natural-science list)
- The Allen School's intro sequence is taught in Java and they strongly prefer prior college-level
  Java. A Prerequisite Exception Request Form exists, due by the departmental deadline.
- Typical admit has ~2 years of coursework and a **3.8+ GPA in the most recent prerequisite courses**.

Also: applicants who already hold a bachelor's degree are not accepted.

## ACMS — Applied and Computational Mathematical Sciences, B.S. (College of Arts & Sciences)
Source: https://acms.washington.edu/admissions
Jointly offered by Applied Mathematics, CSE, Mathematics, and Statistics. You apply to a specific
OPTION (track) and cannot switch options later without reapplying.

Prerequisites (effective Autumn 2025) — minimum 2.0 in each and **overall minimum 3.0 GPA** across them:
- CSE 123 or CSE 143
- MATH 124, MATH 125, MATH 126
- MATH 208 (matrix algebra)
- (MATH 134/135/136 may replace 124/125/126 + 208)
- ONE option core course:
  - Data Science & Statistics -> MATH/STAT 394 Probability I
  - Discrete Mathematical Algorithms -> MATH 300 Introduction to Mathematical Reasoning
  - Mathematical Econ / Quantitative Finance -> MATH/STAT 394 Probability I
  - Scientific Computing -> AMATH 352 Applied Linear Algebra and Numerical Analysis
Rules:
- **No prerequisite course may be in progress at the time of application.**
- Courses must be numerically graded, not S/NS. AP/IB may count toward prerequisites.
- Post-baccalaureate students are not eligible.
Transfer applicants: apply to BOTH UW and ACMS for the same quarter; need at least **30 graded
college credits** by the deadline. UW transfer application by **Feb 15**; ACMS application
**March 30 – April 5**; decisions late May / early June. Appeals for direct admission are not accepted.
Capacity: ACMS admits 50–75 students per cycle; the Data Science & Statistics track is the most
competitive; capacity is expected for most qualified DMA / MEQF / SCNA applicants.
