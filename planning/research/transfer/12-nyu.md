# New York University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## University-wide
Source: https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html
- Common Application for Transfer Students (NYC campus only; Abu Dhabi and Shanghai take no transfers).
- Deadlines: **Spring transfer Oct 15** (decisions Nov–Jan); **Summer/Fall transfer March 15**
  (decisions May–June). Rolling review; earlier is stronger.
- "NYU's evaluation will focus primarily on your college/university grades. Our most competitive
  applicants have completed at least one full-time year of college by the time they enroll."
- **NYU does not award NYU scholarships to transfer applicants** (federal/state aid only).

## College of Arts and Science (CAS)
Source: https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/additional-program-requirements.html
- CAS accepts external transfers for BOTH fall and spring.
- **You must declare a major when you apply.**
- CAS cannot consider transfer applicants for its dual programs in engineering and dentistry.

## Tandon School of Engineering
Same source:
- All applicants must have completed at least a **precalculus-level math course** in high school or
  college. "Our most competitive applicants have completed at least calculus at a college level."
- Physics is REQUIRED only for Applied Physics, Applied Physics & Mathematics, and Mechanical
  Engineering; **recommended for all other majors** (including ECE).
- Chemistry is REQUIRED only for Biomolecular Science and Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering;
  recommended for all other majors.
- **All applicants must submit prerequisite course information through the NYU Applicant Portal.**
  Delay in submitting it delays the application review.
=> Tandon publishes NO hard course list beyond precalculus. This is one of the least prescriptive
   engineering transfer targets on the user's list.

## Majors requested — resolution notes
1. **Electrical and Computer Engineering** — Tandon offers separate **Electrical Engineering B.S.**
   and **Computer Engineering B.S.**; the department is Electrical and Computer Engineering.
   Use the Tandon requirements above.
2. **"Math and CS (Tandon)"** — the joint **Mathematics and Computer Science B.A. is a CAS/Courant
   program, not a Tandon program.** Requirements: ten MATH-UA courses and eight CSCI-UA courses;
   the prerequisite for declaring is CSCI-UA 101 Intro to Computer Science or CSCI-UA 102 Data
   Structures (depending on placement) with **C or better**.
   Tandon separately offers a Mathematics B.S. and a Computer Science B.S., but not a joint major.
   ZotPath resolution: model the exact CAS/Courant joint major and identify its admitting unit;
   do not relabel it as Tandon.
3. **"Statistics"** — NYU has **no standalone undergraduate Statistics major**. Closest options:
   - CAS/Center for Data Science: **Data Science B.A.**
   - CAS: **Data Science and Mathematics B.A.** (18 courses / 72 credits, requires a full sequence of
     upper-level probability and upper-level statistics)
   - Stern: B.S. in Business with a **Statistics** concentration (separate, business-school admission)
   ZotPath resolution: model the two CAS data-science options as the closest academic match. Do not
   silently add Stern's business-school concentration.
