# Yale University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.
Sources: https://admissions.yale.edu/transfer , /transfer-requirements , /transfer-eligibility

## Process
- Yale welcomes a small group of transfer students to enroll as **sophomores or juniors**.
- **Transfer Common Application, deadline March 1. Fall entry only.**
- Required: two letters of recommendation **from instructors who taught you in credit-bearing
  courses on a college campus**, official college AND high school transcripts, the Common App
  College Report, the Common App Transfer Mid-term Report, and **standardized test results
  (ACT or SAT) — required, not optional**.

## *** Credit rules — the most concrete grade gate on the user's list ***
- Yale counts **course credits**, not credit hours: one credit per term course; 36 credits to graduate.
- **A transfer student may transfer at most 18 Yale course credits and must complete at least
  18 course credits at Yale.**
- **"Transfer students must achieve a letter grade of A or B in at least three-quarters of
  transferable non-Yale courses, with no grade lower than C in the remainder."**
- Pass/Fail courses do not typically transfer.
- Transferable subjects: courses similar to those taught in Yale College — English, math
  (statistics, **calculus I and above**), psychology, biology, computer science, physics, history,
  economics, political science, chemistry, non-English languages, philosophy.
  **Not transferable:** communications, criminal justice, business, nursing, military fields.
- **Yale awards NO transfer credit for AP, IB, CLEP, or college credits earned while in high school.**
- Online coursework: eligible only under limited conditions (regular synchronous interaction with
  the instructor and regular feedback); may not satisfy a distributional requirement; at most
  **two asynchronous online course credits** may be approved.
- No transfer credit review happens before admission; admitted students get an estimate at admission.

## Major-related rules
- Yale College students declare a major by the end of sophomore year. Most majors are open to
  transfers, "but some departments have specific rules or prerequisites that may prevent some
  transfer students from pursuing the major within their available semesters."
- **"Prospective transfer students who intend to major in engineering should plan to complete a full
  sequence of college-level single-variable calculus (or higher) and a full sequence of
  college-level introductory physics prior to transferring."** — this applies to the user's EECS target.
- Transfers granted second-semester sophomore or junior standing who want a major requiring a
  sophomore-year application should contact that major's DUS after being admitted.
- Distributional requirements apply to everyone: two course credits each in humanities & arts,
  sciences, and social sciences; two each in quantitative reasoning and writing; one to three for
  foreign language proficiency. Transfer credits may satisfy these, as determined by the
  Associate Dean of Academic Affairs.

## Majors requested (all Yale College — one admission target)
- **Computer Science, B.A./B.S.**
- **Computer Science and Mathematics, B.S.** (joint major)
- **Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, B.S.** (engineering — see the calculus/physics rule)
- **Mathematics, B.A./B.S.**
- **Statistics and Data Science, B.A./B.S.**
