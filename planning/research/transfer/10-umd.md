# University of Maryland, College Park — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## University-wide transfer rules
Source: https://www.admissions.umd.edu/apply/admission-requirements/transfer-applicant-requirements
- Minimum 12 semester (18 quarter) hours at a regionally accredited institution; 30 credits recommended.
- Deadlines: **March 1** for fall, **October 15** for spring.
- At most **90 transferable semester credits** may apply toward a UMD degree.
- Competitive applicants have 30+ post-high-school credits and a 3.0+ GPA (3.5+ fares best).

## Computer Science — a Limited Enrollment Program (LEP)
Sources: https://lep.umd.edu/computerscience-after2024.html ,
         https://undergrad.cs.umd.edu/external-transfer-applicants
- Admission to UMD and admission to the CS major are separate reviews inside one application.
- Transfer students **must complete the Gateway requirements BEFORE applying** to the university,
  then face a selective review. Meeting the Gateway bar does NOT guarantee admission.
- Admitted to UMD but Gateway not finished => placed in the department of Letters and Sciences,
  not in CS.

### Gateway requirements — students matriculating Fall 2024 or later (this applies to the user)
- CMSC 131 **or** CMSC 133 **or** CMSC 141 — minimum grade **B-**
- CMSC 132 **or** CMSC 142 — minimum grade **B-**
- MATH 140 — minimum grade **B-**
- Minimum GPA **3.0** across ALL courses taken at UMD and every other institution

(Pre-Fall-2024 matriculants use C- minimums and a 2.7 GPA. The user starts college Fall 2026, so
the B- / 3.0 standard applies.)

### LEP policies that constrain planning
- Only ONE gateway course may be repeated to earn the required grade, and only once — including
  attempts at other institutions. Taking a second course from an "or" list counts as a repeat.
  A grade of **W counts as an attempt**.
- **A student may apply only once to an LEP.**
- Selective review also weighs academic record, co-curricular involvement, computing research or
  personal projects, and college-level honors.
- Decisions: late April for fall, late October for spring (if Gateways were already complete).
- Post-admission benchmark review after the second semester in the major or 75 cumulative credits:
  CMSC 330 (C-), CMSC 351 (C-), one STAT 4XX or MATH/AMSC/STAT course with MATH 141 prerequisite (C-),
  and a 2.0 cumulative GPA.
- Exemption exams exist for CMSC 131/132 but must be taken before classes start at UMD.
