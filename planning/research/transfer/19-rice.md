# Rice University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.
Source: https://admission.rice.edu/apply/transfer-students

## Definition and eligibility
- You are a transfer applicant if you hold a high school diploma AND have earned at least
  **12 semester hours of college credit after high school**. You may not reduce your credits to
  qualify as a first-year applicant — transcripts of ALL academic work are required.
- **Minimum 3.2 GPA in the most recent college work is required to be considered.**
  Admitted transfers typically have **3.5 or higher**.
- **A transfer student must complete at least four semesters (60 semester hours) full-time at Rice**
  to earn a Rice bachelor's degree.

## *** Key finding ***
> "Transfer applicants do not have specific coursework requirements based on their intended area of
>  study at Rice. However the Admission Committee will consider an applicant's pursuit of and
>  performance in relevant coursework."
Rice is the clearest example on the user's list of a target with **no major-specific course gate** —
only GPA, rigor, and relevance.

## Deadlines and entry terms
- **Fall entry: apply by March 15**, decisions rolling in June.
- **Spring entry: apply by October 15**, decisions by December 1.
- All admission decisions are final; denied applicants may reapply next cycle.

## Application materials
- Common Application + Rice writing supplement, $75 fee
- Official final high school transcript (9th–12th grade), official transcripts from every college
- **College Report**
- **TWO recommendation letters, both from college instructors**
- Testing: **recommended but not required**. Rice superscores and will consider whichever of SAT/ACT
  helps most. AP/IB/AICE scores may be self-reported and may earn course credit after enrolling.

## Transfer credit
- The Office of the Registrar evaluates coursework from regionally accredited institutions.
- **No preliminary transfer credit evaluation before admission.** Admitted students receive a
  preliminary evaluation comparing each course to its Rice equivalent.

## Majors requested
- **Artificial Intelligence, B.S.** — Department of Computer Science, George R. Brown School of
  Engineering and Computing. Distinct pathway from CS; required math courses, CS core, AI core,
  and AI electives. Source: https://ga.rice.edu/programs-study/departments-programs/engineering/artificial-intelligence/artificial-intelligence-bs/
- **Computer Science, B.A. / B.S.** — same department.
- **Electrical and Computer Engineering, B.S.** — ECE department.
- **Mathematics, B.A.** — School of Humanities/Natural Sciences. (Rice also has
  **Computational and Applied Mathematics (CAAM), B.A./B.S.** as the applied counterpart.)
Admission is to Rice, not to a specific major, so all four are effectively one admission decision.
