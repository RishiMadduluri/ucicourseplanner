# University of Michigan, Ann Arbor — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## Application logistics
Source: https://admissions.umich.edu/apply/transfer-applicants/requirements-deadlines
- Common Application; the Office of Undergraduate Admissions reviews transfers for LSA and Engineering.
- Deadlines: **Feb 1** for fall term (also spring/summer half terms); Oct 1 for winter term.
- Final official high school record is required in addition to all college transcripts.
- An explanation is required for any W, I, or gap in education.
- List of all current and future courses must be submitted.

## College of Engineering (CoE)
- **Minimum GPA 3.0**; prerequisites must be "completed", "in progress", or "planned" at the time
  of application, and all must be finished with a letter grade of **C or better by the intended
  term of enrollment**. Completed-before-applying is the most competitive.
- Admission prerequisites (direct-equivalent credit required):
  - Calculus I (U-M MATH 115)
  - Calculus II (U-M MATH 116)
  - One calculus-based physics + lab (PHYSICS 140 + 141, or 240 + 241)
  - One general college chemistry + lab (CHEM 130 + 125 + 126, or CHEM 210 + 211)
  - One computing course (EECS 183 or ENGR 101; **ENGR 101 or C++ preferred**)
  - One first-year English composition
- **AP trap: if AP credit is awarded for Calculus I and/or Calculus II, then Calculus III
  (MATH 215) becomes a requirement.**
- **Departmental credit is not accepted for transfer admission** — the course must map to a
  specific U-M course in the Transfer Credit Equivalency Database, not to generic departmental credit.
- Prerequisites cannot be satisfied by placement exams administered at other institutions, and any
  AP/IB credit must meet U-M's own thresholds regardless of what your current school granted.
- Meeting the GPA and prerequisites does not guarantee admission.

## Computer Science — two colleges, one major name
Source: https://cse.engin.umich.edu/academics/undergraduate/admissions/external-transfers-to-cs
- CS is offered as **CS-LSA** (College of Literature, Science, and the Arts) and **CS-Eng**
  (College of Engineering). A transfer applicant must pick one college.
- **CS-Eng required prerequisites (C or better):** Calculus I, Calculus II, one calculus-based
  physics + lab, one general college chemistry + lab, one computing course (ENGR 101 or C++
  preferred), one English composition. Minimum GPA 3.0.
- **CS-LSA recommended prerequisites:** two semesters of calculus, one semester of computer
  programming. Completing them does not guarantee admission to the major.
- **Advance selection:** applicants must indicate on the Common Application that they want to be
  considered for advance selection into CS. **Transfer students cannot apply for or switch into CS
  once on campus.** Advance-selected students must still meet their college's declaration
  requirements before officially declaring CS.

## Computer Engineering and Electrical Engineering (CoE)
Both are CoE majors and use the CoE admission prerequisite list above. There is no separate
published transfer course list beyond the college prerequisites; department GPA expectations vary
and may change.

## Mathematics and Applied Mathematics (LSA)
- LSA does not publish major-specific admission prerequisites; applicants are admitted to LSA and
  declare afterward. Competitive applicants have a strong transferable curriculum that aligns with
  the intended major and shows upper-level progression.
- Typical competitive applicant has **two or more semesters of transferable graded college work**;
  no fixed credit minimum. The closer to 60 transferable credits, the less high school work matters.
- U-M's Mathematics B.S. carries concentrations, including **Applied Mathematics** — the user's
  "UMich Math" and "UMich Applied Math" are one admission target (LSA) with two in-major programs.
