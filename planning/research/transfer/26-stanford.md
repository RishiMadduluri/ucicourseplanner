# Stanford University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.
Sources: https://admission.stanford.edu/apply/transfer/index.html , /eligibility.html , /credit.html

Stanford is on the **quarter system**, like UCI, and states its unit cap in quarter units.

## Eligibility
- Anyone who has completed courses for college credit in an associate's or bachelor's degree program
  since finishing high school must apply as a transfer.
- Transfer coursework must be at an **accredited degree-granting institution**; vocational,
  technical, performance, and professional program coursework is not considered.
- Dual-enrolled high school students apply for **first-year** admission.
- Holders of a bachelor's degree are ineligible.
- **Students are limited to a total of THREE applications for undergraduate admission to Stanford**,
  first-year and transfer combined.

## *** No required courses; no major-specific admission ***
> "There are no required courses for admission to Stanford."
> "When you apply to Stanford, you apply to the university as a whole, not to a particular major,
>  department, or school. As a result, there are no additional requirements for certain areas of study."
> "If you have attended college for more than one year, we expect that you have begun to focus on a
>  potential major or area of study."
Stanford also says it "cannot provide specific guidance on course selection."

## What the strongest applicants look like
- The equivalent of **two years of college-level coursework by the time they enter Stanford**
- **Consecutive years of full-time, IN-PERSON college study** in an associate or bachelor's program
- Typically in their **second year** of full-time in-person college work when applying

## Transfer credit
- **Stanford has no articulation agreements with any college or institution.**
- A course generally transfers if it is at an accredited institution, **substantially similar to a
  Stanford course**, completed with a **minimum grade of C- or Pass (where Pass = C- or above)**,
  does not duplicate/overlap/regress prior coursework, and was **not used toward high school
  graduation requirements**.
- **Maximum 90 QUARTER UNITS transferable** (about two years) regardless of units earned elsewhere.
- **At least two years of full-time enrollment at Stanford** are required for the bachelor's degree.
- Online courses: "Some Stanford departments and general education requirements do not accept any
  online courses." Extension/correspondence/online credit transfers only if the offering university
  allows it toward its own degree.

## Deadlines and required materials
- **Common Application due March 15**; missing-document notice in April; decision by mid-May;
  reply June 1.
- **ACT or SAT test scores are REQUIRED.**
- Official high school transcript with graduation date; official transcripts from every college;
  College Report; **letters of recommendation from two academic instructors**; $100 fee.

## Majors requested (all one admission target — the university)
- Computer Science, B.S. (School of Engineering)
- **"Data Science"** — Stanford's closest degrees are **Mathematical and Computational Science, B.S.**
  and **Statistics: Data Science, B.S.**; there is no B.S. literally named "Data Science."
- Electrical Engineering, B.S.
- Mathematics, B.S.
- Statistics, B.S.
