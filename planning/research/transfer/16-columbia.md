# Columbia University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.
Source: https://undergrad.admissions.columbia.edu/apply/transfer

## Eligibility and process
- Columbia College and Columbia Engineering together welcome **over 125 transfer students each year**.
- Application: **Coalition Application on Scoir** + Columbia supplement. **Deadline March 1.**
  Decisions by June 1. **Fall entry only — no spring transfer applications are accepted.**
- Must have earned **24 points of credit** (one year of full-time study), or be registered to
  complete 24 points by enrollment, plus a high school diploma by the deadline.
- **Candidates who have completed more than four semesters of college coursework elsewhere are not
  encouraged to apply** — Columbia says finishing all graduation requirements in a reasonable time
  becomes unlikely.
- **Minimum GPA expectation: 3.5 overall.** Columbia Engineering candidates are strongly encouraged
  to have a **3.5 in mathematics and science courses**.
- Transfer admit rate is typically **under 10%**.
- Required: high school transcript from all high schools, college transcripts from all colleges,
  Coalition Transfer Report, Coalition Curriculum Report, **two college academic recommendations**,
  and **standardized tests** (SAT/ACT required for the 2027-28 cycle and beyond).
- Because of timing, Columbia generally cannot see second-semester grades from the application year,
  so **high school grades, rigor, and test scores matter a great deal**, especially for sophomore entry.
- Online courses: in-person is preferred, but online classes accepted for credit by your home
  institution can count — **up to 3 online classes for sophomore-year entry, up to 6 for junior-year
  entry** (semester system; equivalent policy for quarter systems).
- Enrolling transfers must still complete the **Core Curriculum** on top of major requirements, and
  review considers whether the degree can be finished within a standard eight semesters.
  Columbia therefore recommends applying with **first-year standing** where possible.
- Switching between Columbia College and Columbia Engineering after enrolling requires a separate
  **Internal Transfer Application by March 1**, and is not guaranteed.

## Columbia Engineering (SEAS) — CS, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Applied Mathematics
"The majority of successful applicants to Columbia Engineering have completed an engineering track
curriculum. For applicants with first-year standing at time of application this includes:"
- **Two semesters of Calculus**
- **Two semesters of Physics**
- **One semester of Chemistry**

Degree homes:
- **Computer Science B.S.** — SEAS (8 foundational courses + a 7-course track; ~74–75 points)
- **Computer Engineering B.S.** — joint Computer Science / Electrical Engineering program in SEAS
- **Electrical Engineering B.S.** — SEAS
- **Applied Mathematics B.S.** — SEAS, Department of Applied Physics and Applied Mathematics (APAM);
  the major requires 38–43 points and covers calculus, discrete math, differential equations,
  linear algebra, and probability/statistics.

## Columbia College — Mathematics, Computer Science
- **Mathematics B.A.** and **Computer Science B.A.** (7 foundational courses + a 5–6 course track,
  40–47 points), plus the joint **Computer Science–Mathematics B.A.**
- Columbia College publishes no separate transfer course prerequisites; the Core Curriculum is the
  dominant constraint.
- Columbia College applicants with a break in education of more than a year (other than national
  military service), or who want part-time study, are directed to the School of General Studies
  instead. This restriction does NOT apply to Columbia Engineering applicants.
