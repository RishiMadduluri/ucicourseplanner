# University of Pennsylvania — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## University-wide
Sources: https://admissions.upenn.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/requirements-deadlines ,
         https://admissions.upenn.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/eligibility-credit-guidelines
- Eligible with **one to two years** of transferable academic coursework at another college.
- **All admitted students (first-year and transfer) start in the FALL.** No mid-year enrollment.
- Transfer application deadline **March 15** (application opens Jan 5).
- **Penn REQUIRES SAT or ACT scores from transfer applicants** (2025-26 cycle onward), taken within
  the past five years. A hardship waiver can be submitted through the application.
- **Two-year academic enrollment requirement**: at least HALF of your total courses must be
  completed at Penn, regardless of prior credit.
- At least one full year of transferable coursework (**8 semester courses or equivalent**) must be
  finished by the spring term before enrollment. Summer coursework immediately before enrolling
  usually does not count toward those 8.
- Transfer credit criteria: regionally accredited, **grade of C or better**, similar in content and
  credit hours to a Penn course, **at least 40 contact hours per term**. Grades do not transfer or
  affect the Penn GPA. Departments grant credit course-by-course after matriculation.
- **Penn does not grant credit for dual-enrollment coursework.**
- Students who already hold a bachelor's degree may not transfer.
- Also required: mid-term report (if entering as a second-year), professor recommendation,
  College Report submitted directly by the institution, four Penn-specific essays.
- Pass/Fail coursework needs evidence that performance was C or better to earn credit.

## School of Engineering and Applied Science (SEAS)
Source: https://admissions.upenn.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/penn-engineering
- **Transfer applicants must select a major on the application.**
- Expected academic preparation:
  - One course in chemistry
  - One course in physics (**involving the use of calculus**)
  - One course in computer programming
  - **Two courses in calculus**
  - If possible, two courses in social sciences and humanities
- **A second physics course** is expected for bioengineering, **computer engineering**, mechanical
  engineering and applied mechanics, or materials science.
- A second chemistry course is expected for bioengineering, chemical & biomolecular engineering, or
  materials science.
- Degrees: **BSE** (professional/technical) and **BAS** (technology + liberal arts). CIS offers
  Computer Science BSE and BAS; ESE offers **Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, and
  Artificial Intelligence** BSE degrees (37 course units each).
- *** Penn has NO degree literally named "Computer Science and Engineering." *** ZotPath resolves
  the user's Penn "CSE" to separate Computer Science BSE (CIS) and Computer Engineering BSE (ESE)
  cards so the user can compare both exact degrees.

## College of Arts and Sciences (CAS)
Source: https://admissions.upenn.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/college-arts-sciences
- Accepts second-year and third-year transfers with up to two years of credit.
- Natural-science and economics majors: "you will need exposure to college-level calculus to
  successfully transfer into these programs."
- Total for graduation is 32–36 course units; a major is never fewer than 12 course units.
- **Mathematics is a B.A.** The only named concentration is Biological Mathematics; Penn has
  **no undergraduate Applied Mathematics major or concentration**. ZotPath keeps Mathematics B.A.
  as the closest academic target and explicitly states that this is not an Applied Math degree.

## Wharton School — the user's "Wharton Statistics"
Source: https://admissions.upenn.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/wharton
- Wharton awards ONE degree: the **B.S. in Economics** (37 course units). "Statistics" is a
  **concentration** (four upper-level courses), not a separate major. You indicate the
  concentration when you apply.
- Typically admits only students with one year of credit, entering as second-years.
- Expected preparation:
  - **At least one Calculus course completed in the first year of university study** (more may be
    required after credit evaluation); pre-college math and test scores may also be considered
  - **Both introductory macroeconomics AND microeconomics** — coursework, or 5s on both AP
    Macro and AP Micro, or a 6/7 on IB Higher Level Economics
  - Recommended: satisfy Wharton's foreign-language requirement (two semesters of college-level
    language, or placement/test scores) before enrolling
