# Carnegie Mellon University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## External transfer process (verified)
Source: https://www.cmu.edu/admission/admission/transfer-applicants
- Transfer applicants are considered for exactly ONE college or program. No alternate choice.
- Must have completed at least one semester of college before the deadline.
- Fall deadline Feb 16 (documents Mar 1, decisions by May 17); spring deadline Oct 15.
- Every college EXCEPT College of Fine Arts accepts fall transfers "if space is available."
- BXA intercollege programs do NOT accept external transfers.
- Requires: college transcript, HIGH SCHOOL transcript, course descriptions from every college
  attended, professor/adviser recommendation, Common App College Report.
- University housing is not guaranteed for transfer students.
- CMU publishes NO college-level course prerequisite list for external transfer applicants. The
  published "academic requirements" page is a HIGH SCHOOL unit table (4 yr English, 4 yr math
  through pre-calc with calculus strongly encouraged, 1 yr physics, etc.).
  Source: https://cmu.edu/admission/admission/academic-requirements-college-level-work

## School of Computer Science (CS, AI, Computational Biology, Robotics, HCI)
Source: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/academics/undergraduate/admissions
- CMU admits into SCS, NOT directly into a department. The department chosen on the application
  "does not factor in the admissions process."
- Major is declared in the SPRING SEMESTER of the first year as an SCS student, choosing among
  Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Computational Biology, Robotics, Human-Computer Interaction.
  => The user's "CMU CS" and "CMU Robotics" are the SAME admission target (SCS).
- "Extremely few external transfers are admitted to SCS due to space limitations."
- Evaluation: seat availability, overall academic performance, course rigor at the current
  institution, ability to finish the SCS program on time, recommendations, reflection essays.

Internal-transfer bar (best available proxy for the level of preparation SCS expects):
Source: https://csd.cmu.edu/guidelines-for-internal-transfer-or-dual-degree
- >= 3.6 QPA across 15-122, 15-150, 15-210, 15-213, 15-251, 21-127, and >= 3.0 overall QPA.
Robotics primary major internal bar: 21-127, 15-122, 15-213, 15-251, one of 16-211/16-299/16-311,
one of 36-225/21-325/36-218/15-259, >= 3.6 QPA over those six and >= 3.0 overall.

CS/AI lower-division core to prepare for (source: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/bs-in-artificial-intelligence/curriculum):
- 21-120 Differential and Integral Calculus, 21-122 Integration and Approximation,
  21-259 Calculus in Three Dimensions, 21-241 Matrices and Linear Transformations
- 15-151/21-127 Mathematical Foundations / Concepts of Mathematics
- 36-218 Probability Theory for Computer Scientists
- 15-112, 15-122, 15-150, 15-210, 15-213, 15-251

## College of Engineering — ECE
Source: https://engineering.cmu.edu/education/academic-policies/undergraduate-policies/transferring.html
(This page is the INTERNAL transfer policy; the course bar is the best proxy for external readiness.)
- Minimum grade C in: one introductory engineering course for the target major, the science
  co-requisite, calculus (21-120), and Physics I (33-141 preferred, or 33-151/33-121).
- For Electrical & Computer Engineering the intro course is 18-100 and the co-requisite is
  computer programming 15-110 or 15-112.

## Mellon College of Science — Department of Mathematical Sciences
Source: https://www.cmu.edu/math/undergrad/faq.html
- Degree is "Mathematical Sciences, B.S." with concentrations. CMU has NO separate "Applied
  Mathematics" degree — the user's "CMU Applied Math" = Mathematical Sciences B.S. with the
  Computational and Applied Mathematics concentration.
- HARD TRANSFER-CREDIT RULE: "The Department of Mathematical Sciences does not accept online or
  distance education courses for transfer credit." Courses must be taught in person by faculty at
  an accredited institution.
- Transfer credit requests are routed through the home-department academic adviser.
- Internal major transfer needs B or better in 21-127/21-128.
- Core: 21-120, 21-122, 21-127/21-128, 21-228, 21-241/21-242, 21-259/21-268, 21-260, 15-110.
