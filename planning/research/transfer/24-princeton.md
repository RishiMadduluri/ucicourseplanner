# Princeton University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.
Sources: https://admission.princeton.edu/transfer-students ,
         https://admission.princeton.edu/apply/application-checklist/transfer-application-checklist

## Eligibility
- Fall entry only. **Most transfers enter as SOPHOMORES**; some may enter as juniors, and some may
  be required to enter as first-year students. Standing is set by Princeton faculty and college
  deans after evaluating transfer credit.
- **You may not transfer for higher standing than fall of junior year.**
- Eligible with the equivalent of **one or more years of full-time postsecondary transferable
  credit** by the spring before enrollment. Less than one full-time year => apply as a first-year
  (and then **no course credit transfers at all**).
- Dual-enrolled high school students must apply as first-years; **college credits earned before
  finishing high school cannot transfer to a Princeton degree**.
- Holders of a bachelor's degree are ineligible for transfer AND first-year admission (Princeton
  grants no second bachelor's degrees). An associate's degree does not disqualify you.
- Princeton's transfer program "particularly encourages applications from students from lower-income
  backgrounds, community college students and U.S. military veterans."

## *** Credit rule that matters most ***
> "The fulfillment of Princeton's general education requirements is prioritized in awarding transfer
>  credit. **Generally, departmental requirements for majors and certificate programs may not be
>  fulfilled through transfer credit.**"
So UCI major coursework will most likely count toward Princeton's *general education*, not toward
the CS / ECE / Math concentration itself.

## Application (deadline March 1; decisions mid-May; reply late May)
- Common Application + Princeton-specific Questions
- **Graded Written Paper** from a course in the last two to three years — preferably English or
  history; humanities and social sciences also accepted for transfer applicants
- Official transcripts from ALL postsecondary institutions plus an official high school transcript
- Transfer/College Report; Transfer Mid-Term Report if currently enrolled
- **Two academic recommendations from different academic areas, at least one from a college
  instructor**, in core academic areas (English, language, humanities, social sciences,
  natural sciences, math)
- **Testing: optional for fall 2026 and fall 2027 entry; REQUIRED again for fall 2028 entry
  (2027-28 application cycle).** A student starting at UCI in fall 2026 would apply for fall 2028
  or later — so plan on SAT/ACT being required.

## Majors requested
- **Computer Science** — offered as both an **A.B.** (College) and a **B.S.E.** (Engineering).
- **Electrical and Computer Engineering, B.S.E.**
- **Mathematics, A.B.**
- **"Applied and Computational Mathematics"** — *** Princeton has NO undergraduate ACM major. ***
  PACM offers a **minor** (formerly a certificate): five courses normally at the 300 level or above,
  letter-graded, at least three outside the student's major; plus a not-for-credit colloquium in
  the spring of junior and senior year and an independent research paper.
  Source: https://ua.princeton.edu/fields-study/minors/applied-and-computational-mathematics
  Model the user's Princeton "Applied and Computational Math" as **Mathematics A.B. + the PACM
  minor**, and flag it.
