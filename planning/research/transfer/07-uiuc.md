# University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31. Course lists read directly from the official Transfer Handbook pages.

## Campus-wide
- Transfer applicants are considered for FALL admission only (Grainger).
- Application window: Jan 15 – Apr 5 for fall.
- Minimum GPA varies by program, 2.00 to 3.5.
- Graduation: >= 45 semester hours must be completed at UIUC, >= 21 of them at the 300/400 level.
  LAS degrees need 124 total hours and >= 40 upper-division hours.
- Test-based (AP/IB) credit policies are only finalized after the cycle; UIUC "strongly
  encourages" satisfying admission requirements with graded transferable credit instead.

## Grainger College of Engineering — structural rules
Source: https://transferhandbook.illinois.edu/eng/
- **You must be admitted DIRECTLY into Grainger at the time of transfer.** Transfer students who
  enter any other UIUC college — including Engineering Undeclared — are NOT eligible for later
  on-campus transfer into Grainger.
- Grainger majors cannot be listed as a second-choice major on the application.
- No dual-degree petitions or major-change requests are permitted into Computer Engineering,
  Computer Science, CS+Bioengineering, CS+Physics, Electrical Engineering, or Mechanical Engineering.
- Every required/technical course needs **B or better (3.0)**; repeat anything below a B before applying.
- Expectation of rigor: 3–4 technical courses and 15+ credit hours per semester, with at least one
  such semester completed before applying.

### Computer Science, B.S. (Grainger)
Minimum GPA 3.0. Required before the application term (B or better each):
- CHEM 102 + CHEM 103 (General Chemistry I + lab)
- CS 124 Intro to Computer Science I
- MATH 220 or MATH 221 Calculus I
- MATH 231 Calculus II
- MATH 241 Calculus III
- PHYS 211 Mechanics
- PHYS 212 Electricity & Magnetism
Skills: formal coursework in at least TWO of Python, Java, C++.
Recommended: CS 128, CS 173 or MATH 213 (discrete), CS 225 data structures,
MATH 257/225/415/416 linear algebra.
Note: applicants entering with 50+ hours who have not met these will take longer to graduate;
UIUC suggests exploring CS + X in LAS instead.

### Computer Engineering, B.S. (Grainger)
Minimum GPA 3.0. Required (B or better each):
- CHEM 102 + CHEM 103
- MATH 220/221, MATH 231, MATH 241
- PHYS 211, PHYS 212
- ONE of: CS 124, or ECE 110 Introduction to Electronics (or ECE 205 if 110 unavailable)
Recommended: CS 124, CS 128, ECE 110/205, MATH 285 (or 284), MATH 257 or 416,
PHYS 213, PHYS 214, CS 173 or MATH 213, CS 225. Python familiarity recommended.

### Electrical Engineering, B.S. (Grainger)
Identical required list to Computer Engineering (GPA 3.0; CHEM 102+103, MATH 220/221, 231, 241,
PHYS 211, PHYS 212, and one of CS 124 / ECE 110 / ECE 205).
Recommended: CS 124, CS 128, CS 173 or MATH 213, CS 225, ECE 110/205, MATH 285/284,
MATH 257 or 416, PHYS 213, PHYS 214.

## College of Liberal Arts & Sciences

### Mathematics & Computer Science, B.S.  (= the user's "CS + Math")
Source: https://transferhandbook.illinois.edu/las/mathematics-computer-science/
- Minimum GPA **3.5**.
- **Minimum grade of A- required in each Computer Science and Mathematics course taken.**
- Fewer than 50 transferable hours at application: CS 124 (or CS 101 if no CS 124 equivalent),
  MATH 220, MATH 231.
- 50 or more transferable hours at application: the above PLUS MATH 241 Calculus III,
  CS 173 or MATH 213 (discrete), CS 225 Data Structures.
- Recommended: CS 128, Composition I (RHET 105 — usually a two-course English composition sequence).
- Language other than English: Level 4 for graduation (not required for admission).
