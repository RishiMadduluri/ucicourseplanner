# UC Berkeley — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## System-wide UC-to-UC transfer facts
Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/admission-requirements/transfer-requirements/preparing-to-transfer/other-types-of-transfers.html
- Current UC students are already "UC-eligible" and are NOT required to complete the 7-course pattern.
- Must be in good standing (last-term and/or cumulative UC GPA >= 2.00) and competitive GPA.
- All courses/grades/units earned at a UC campus transfer to another UC campus (some UC Extension excepted).
- UC GPA follows the student; departments decide how credits apply to MAJOR requirements.
- Completing the 7-course pattern is "strongly encouraged" — may be a selection factor.
- CCC students have FIRST PRIORITY over all other transfer applicants at every UC.

## Berkeley-wide
- Junior transfer only: 60–80 semester units (= 90–120 quarter units) completed by end of spring prior to fall.
- Fall entry only; no spring transfer. UC application filing window Nov 1–30.
- "UC does not maintain articulation agreements from any four-year institution."
  Course equivalency evaluated case-by-case via syllabus review AFTER admission. No guarantee.
  Source: https://eecs.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate/eecs-bs/4-year-transfers/
- L&S and CDSS applicants do NOT file a major-prerequisite form; CoE/other colleges do.
  Source: https://admissions.berkeley.edu/apply-to-berkeley/transfer-students/transfer-applicant-checklist/

## Majors requested

### 1. EECS, B.S. — College of Engineering
Source: https://eecs.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate/eecs-bs/4-year-transfers/
Required core prep, ALL completed by spring prior to fall enrollment:
- One year of calculus (Math 1A/1B → now Math 51/52)
- Multivariable calculus (Math 53)
- Differential equations AND linear algebra (Math 54)
- Two calculus-based physics courses WITH LAB (Physics 7A, 7B)
- One additional natural science (chem, physics or bio)
- Two reading & composition courses (R&C A + B)
GPA: technical + overall GPA are the main criteria; 3.5+ recommended.
Excessive upper-division coursework makes an applicant INELIGIBLE.

### 2. Computer Science, B.A. — CDSS (was L&S)
Source: https://eecs.berkeley.edu/resources/undergrads/cs/faq-4/ , https://cdss.berkeley.edu/admissions
- Lower-division CS core: CS 61A, CS 61B, CS 61C. Math 1A/1B (51/52) + Math 54.
- CS 70 (discrete math/probability): Berkeley does NOT accept transfer credit for CS 70.
- "It is very rare that Berkeley accepts any course offered elsewhere as a substitute for CS 61A or 61C."
- Transfers guaranteed five semesters (2.5 yr) in CDSS; advised to take only 2 technical courses/semester at first.
- IMPORTANT: transfer students admitted Fall 2024 or later may NOT switch their major TO computer science
  and may not pursue a simultaneous degree. You must be admitted directly to CS.

### 3. Data Science, B.A. — CDSS  (not on user's Berkeley list but relevant sibling)
Source: https://cdss.berkeley.edu/dsus/prospectivetransfers
- REQUIRED before transfer: Calc I (Math 1A/51), Calc II (Math 1B/52), Linear Algebra (Math 54 or 56).
- Strongly recommended: DATA C8, CS 61A, CS 61B.
- Must select Data Science on the application to be admitted directly to the major.

### 4. Mathematics, B.A. and 5. Applied Mathematics, B.A. — College of Letters & Science
Source: https://math.berkeley.edu/undergraduate/major/pure , /applied ,
        https://math.berkeley.edu/undergraduate/major/frequently-asked-questions
- Transfers admitted to Berkeley who selected Applied Math or Math on the application are
  admitted DIRECTLY into the major (no separate declaration).
- Lower-division prep for both: MATH 51 (was 1A), 52 (was 1B), 53, 54 (or 56), 55.
- Declaration standard for students already at Berkeley: grade C or better in each of
  51, 52, 53, 54/56, 55 — or two of {53, 54, 55} with C average in 51-52 for provisional declaration.

### "EE" as a standalone major
Berkeley has NO standalone Electrical Engineering B.S. EE is part of the EECS B.S. in the
College of Engineering. Model the user's "Berkeley EE" as EECS B.S. and say so in the UI.
