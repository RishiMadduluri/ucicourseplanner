# University of Virginia — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## University-wide
Source: https://admission.virginia.edu/transfer/about-transfer/transfer
- **Minimum eligibility: 24 transferable semester hours completed or in progress, and one full year
  removed from high school graduation by the time of entry.**
- Successful applicants ordinarily have a **cumulative GPA of B or better** and have completed (or
  nearly completed) the specific transfer course requirements for their intended school.
- **Admission is granted to a SCHOOL, not to a major** — neither the College of Arts & Sciences nor
  the School of Engineering admits directly to a major.
- Deadlines: **Fall admission March 1**; Spring admission **October 1, College of Arts & Sciences only**
  (requires 24 credit hours earned by the end of the preceding fall; first-semester students are
  ineligible for spring).
- **Every transfer student must complete at least four semesters of full-time coursework in
  residence at UVA.**
- Must be in good academic and social standing at every institution attended.
- Requires ALL previous college AND high school transcripts.

## School of Engineering and Applied Science (SEAS) — CS B.S., Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering
Source: https://admission.virginia.edu/transfer/transfer-course-requirements
- Transfer students are admitted to SEAS and **declare a major after paying the admission deposit**,
  when contacted by the Engineering Registrar. So CS/CpE/EE are one admission target.
- SEAS transfer course requirements:
  - **Calculus 2 and Multivariable Calculus**
  - **Physics (8 credits) — two calculus-based college physics courses**
  - **Chemistry (4 credits) — at least one calculus-based college chemistry course**
  - **Computer Science (3 credits) — one introductory programming course, preferably in PYTHON**
  - **English Composition — your school's full course sequence**
  - Differential Equations — recommended, not required
- All core engineering courses must be complete before applying; May–June (fall) applicants may note
  in the application if one or two prerequisites will be finished over the summer.
- Special attention is given to performance in mathematics and science.
- Every SEAS undergraduate writes a thesis in the final year (STS 4500 + STS 4600).
- Chemical Engineering may take three years even for third-year transfers (illustrative of pacing).

## College of Arts & Sciences — Mathematics, Computer Science B.A.
- The College's published transfer course requirements are **general-education literacies, not
  major prerequisites**:
  - R21 "Rhetoric for the 21st Century" (6 credits): first writing course = your school's full
    English composition sequence; the SECOND writing requirement must be completed **in residence at UVA**
  - World Languages: proficiency through the intermediate (202) level
  - Quantification, Computation, and Data Analysis (QCD): two courses, 6–8 credits
  - AIP (3), CMP (3–4), CSW (3), HP (3), LS (3–4), SS (3–4), SES (3)
- UVA Mathematics is a **B.A. with concentrations**; each concentration requires nine 3000+-level
  math courses (~28 credits), needing **C or better in seven of them and C- in the other two**.
  UVA has no separate undergraduate "Applied Mathematics" degree — it is a concentration within
  the Mathematics major. Source: https://math.virginia.edu/undergraduate/requirements/
- UVA also offers a Computer Science **B.A.** through the College (distinct from the SEAS B.S.).
