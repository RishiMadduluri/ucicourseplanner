# Dartmouth College — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## Eligibility
Source: https://admissions.dartmouth.edu/glossary-term/eligibility-guidelines
- Students who have matriculated at a college and completed **two years or fewer** of college
  coursework — even one term or fewer — are **only** eligible for transfer admission.
- Students who already hold a bachelor's degree are not eligible for admission to Dartmouth at all.
- College coursework counted toward high school graduation makes you a **first-year** applicant.

## Process
Source: https://admissions.dartmouth.edu/glossary-term/transfer
- **Deadline March 1**, decision mid-May. Fall entry only.
- Dartmouth uses its **own online transfer application** (not the Common App), fee $90.
- Required: college transcript and College/Dean's Certification, secondary school report,
  **two academic recommendations from college faculty**, Dartmouth transfer essays.
- *** Standardized testing IS a required element of Dartmouth's transfer application. ***
  Source: https://admissions.dartmouth.edu/glossary-question/testing-required-part-transfer-application
  A testing waiver may be requested if you did not test for first-year admission, if scores are more
  than five years old, or if sitting the exams would be a hardship; waiver applications are still
  reviewed.
- No published minimum GPA; successful transfers typically present a very strong record
  (widely reported around 3.7+). Reported recent transfer admit rate ~1.5–2%.

## Majors requested
Dartmouth admits to the College, not to a major, and runs on **quarters (the D-Plan)** — the same
calendar as UCI, which simplifies course mapping.
- **Computer Science, A.B.** (Dartmouth also offers a B.E. through Thayer)
- **Mathematics, A.B.**
No major-specific transfer course requirements are published.
