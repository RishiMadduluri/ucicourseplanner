## Samueli School of Engineering (HSSEAS)
Sources: https://www.seasoasa.ucla.edu/undergraduate-admissions/ ,
         https://www.seasoasa.ucla.edu/hsseas-transfer-requirements/ ,
         https://admission.ucla.edu/apply/transfer/deciding-on-major/major-preparation-engineering
- Minimum 3.4 GPA in all college work at time of application.
- Junior standing by entry: 60 semester / 90 quarter transferable units.
- Two transferable English composition courses, one equivalent to UCLA Eng Comp 3.
- All courses applied to the major must be LETTER GRADED.
- Grade repeat policy: if a major-prep course is repeated, BOTH the original and the new grade count.
- "Missing even one major prep course will likely result in a denial if the applicant pool is competitive."
- HARD RULE: "It is not possible for transfer students, even those admitted as engineering majors,
  to change into the majors of Computer Science, Computer Science & Engineering, Aerospace, or
  Mechanical Engineering." And transfers admitted to a non-engineering major may never move into HSSEAS.
  => Must be admitted DIRECTLY into CS or CSE.

### Required prep — Computer Science B.S. and Computer Science and Engineering B.S.
- Calculus I and II; Calculus III (multivariable); Differential equations; Linear algebra
- Calculus-based physics WITH LABS: (1) mechanics, (2) electricity & magnetism,
  (3) waves/sound/heat/optics/modern physics
- Chemistry: NONE required for CS and CS&E
- Programming: C++ recommended (C, C++ or Java accepted; C++ preferred for competitiveness)

### Required prep — Electrical Engineering B.S. (and Computer Engineering B.S.)
- Same math and physics as above
- Electrical Engineering additionally requires ONE general chemistry course with lab
- Computer Engineering: no chemistry
- Programming: C++ preferred

## College of Letters and Science
Source: https://admission.ucla.edu/apply/transfer/deciding-on-major/major-preparation-the-college/...

### Mathematics majors (includes Applied Mathematics, Mathematics of Computation)
- REQUIRED: 1.5 years of calculus through multivariable; AND linear algebra OR differential equations.
- STRONGLY RECOMMENDED: full calculus sequence plus BOTH linear algebra and differential equations,
  and one introductory C++ course.
- Financial Actuarial Mathematics and Mathematics/Economics are highly selective (not requested).
- Students stay "pre-major" until lower-division prep is finished at UCLA.
- UCLA Applied Math pre-major course list (from the department): MATH 31A, 31B, 32A, 32B, 33A, 33B,
  PHYSICS 1A, 1B, PIC 10A, and one of CHEM 20A, 20B or PHYSICS 1C. Math sequence needs >= 2.5 GPA
  and C or better in each.

### Statistics and Data Science B.S. (this is UCLA's "Data Science")
- REQUIRED: one approved introductory statistics course; 1.5 years of calculus through multivariable;
  linear algebra (required for Fall 2026 entry and later).
- Highly selective; demand significantly exceeds space. Pre-Statistics until lower-div done at UCLA.
- Only ASSIST-approved statistics courses satisfy the statistics requirement.

### Data Theory B.S. (alternate "data science" major, joint Math + Statistics)
- REQUIRED: 1.5 years calculus through multivariable; linear algebra OR differential equations;
  one introductory statistics course.
- Strongly recommended: full calc sequence + linear algebra + diff eq, and one intro C++ course.
