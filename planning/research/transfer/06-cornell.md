# Cornell University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

Cornell CS lives in the **Bowers College of Computing and Information Science**, but the DEGREE is
awarded by either the College of Arts & Sciences (B.A.) or the David A. Duffield College of
Engineering (B.S.). "Bowers" is not a separate admission target. The user's three Cornell CS entries
resolve to exactly two admission targets: CS B.A. (A&S) and CS B.S. (Duffield Engineering).

## University transfer calendar
Source: https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants

- Cornell considers transfer applications for **fall only**.
- The application and fee/waiver are due **March 15**.
- All other required materials are due **April 15**.
- SAT/ACT scores are neither required nor expected for transfer candidates, although applicants
  self-report AP, IB, or A-Level scores in the Common Application.

## *** Critical finding ***
Source: https://www.engineering.cornell.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/transfer-applicants/transfer-requirements
> "Duffield Engineering is not accepting transfer applications for Computer Science;
>  Information Science, Systems, and Technology; or Independent Major."
=> Cornell CS **B.S. is CLOSED to external transfer applicants** (2025-26 admissions cycle).
   The only transfer route to Cornell CS is the A&S **B.A.**

## Duffield College of Engineering — transfer rules
- Transfer application and fee deadline: **March 15**; all other required materials: **April 15**.
- Admits transfers at first- or second-semester sophomore, or first-semester junior standing.
- HARD GATE — the "Common Curriculum four": an application is **not even reviewed** unless all four
  are complete or in progress at the time of application:
  1. MATH 1910 Calculus for Engineers (calculus 2 level)
  2. MATH 1920 Multivariable Calculus for Engineers
  3. PHYS 1112 Physics I: Mechanics & Heat **with** PHYS 1110 Intro to Experimental Physics
  4. CHEM 2090 Engineering General Chemistry **with** CHEM 2091 lab
- Transfer credit rules: course must be ~80% similar to the Cornell course; C or better (not C-);
  max 33 credits per year of full-time study elsewhere; max 66 total transfer credits toward
  graduation (including AP/IB); must complete **at least 60 credits at Cornell**;
  quarter-system credits are REDUCED when converted to semester credits;
  MOOCs and non-degree courses ineligible; syllabus + official transcript required.
- Cornell will not honor credit your current school gave you from an exam below Cornell's own
  threshold (e.g. AP Calc BC 4 is not accepted even if your school gave credit).
- Fall 2025 entrants onward: two PE courses + university water competency.

### Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) — accepting transfers
Note from Cornell: successful ECE transfer applicants "will generally have completed introductory
coursework in digital logic design, fundamental properties of circuits, or signal processing."
Sophomore standing:
- MATH 1910, MATH 1920
- PHYS 1112 + PHYS 1110
- CHEM 2090 + CHEM 2091
- One of: ECE/ENGRD 2100 Intro to Circuits, or ENGRD 2300 Digital Logic & Computer Organization
- CS 1112 or CS 1110
- Two First-Year Writing Seminars (3 credits each)
Additional for junior standing:
- One of MATH 2930 (diff eq) or MATH 2940 (linear algebra)
- A second course from {ECE/ENGRD 2100, ECE/ENGRD 2300}
- PHYS 2213 Electromagnetism, PHYS 2214 Oscillations/Waves/Quantum
- Two Liberal Studies courses

## College of Arts & Sciences — transfer rules
Source: https://as.cornell.edu/admissions/transfer
- Must complete a minimum of **60 credits at Cornell** and **four semesters in residency in A&S**.
- Junior transfer applicants must have "fulfilled the prerequisite coursework for your intended major."
- Strongly encouraged to take at least one writing-intensive composition course.
- Contact: as-transfer@cornell.edu

### Computer Science B.A. (A&S) — affiliation ("admission to the major")
Source: https://catalog.cornell.edu/programs/computer-science-ba/
- C or better (not C-) in every CS and critical math course.
- Average >= 2.5 across CS 2110 (or 2112) and CS 2800 (or 2802).
- Average >= 2.3 across MATH 1120 or MATH 1920 plus any other completed critical math
  (MATH 2940, MATH 2210, MATH 2310).
- **Qualifying courses must be taken at Cornell for a letter grade.** A transfer student therefore
  still has to take CS 2110 and CS 2800 at Cornell before affiliating.

### Mathematics B.A. (A&S) — includes the applied-mathematics concentration
Cornell has no separate undergraduate "Applied Mathematics" degree; applied work is a concentration
inside the Mathematics major. Source: https://math.cornell.edu/applying-math-major-transfer-student
Transfer applicants are expected to have:
1. one semester of linear algebra (matrices, linear systems, vector spaces, linear transformations,
   eigenvalues/eigenvectors)
2. one semester of multivariable differential, integral and vector calculus incl. Green's, Stokes',
   and Divergence theorems
3. one introductory computer programming course
- A transfer applicant who performed at a high level in (1) and/or (2) can usually receive transfer
  credit for MATH 2210 and/or 2220 and be admitted to the math major.
- If the programming course is missing, admission to the major is PROVISIONAL and the student must
  take CS 1110/1112/1114/2110 in the first semester.
- Standard (non-transfer) admission bar: B- or better in linear algebra (2210/2230/2940) and in
  multivariable calculus (2220/2240/1920), plus a 3-4 credit programming course at C- or better.
- Courses from another institution may replace at most TWO courses toward the major requirements.
