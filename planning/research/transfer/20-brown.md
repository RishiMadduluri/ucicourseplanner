## *** Quarter-system eligibility — directly relevant to a UCI student ***
Brown states its eligibility windows in BOTH semesters and quarters:
- **Fall entry** (apply in the spring): eligible only if you will have completed
  **2 or 4 semesters, or 3, 4, or 6 QUARTERS** of full-time college enrollment before September.
- **Spring entry** (apply in the fall): eligible only if you will have completed
  **3 semesters or 5 QUARTERS** or the equivalent before January.
- A candidate who will have completed **5 quarters by the time of entry is eligible for SPRING
  entry only**.
- **Summer terms do not count** toward eligibility unless your institution required them.
- Spring entry is open to **U.S. citizens and permanent residents only**; fall entry is open to all.

## Hard limits
- Anyone admitted as a transfer must be **enrolled full-time at Brown for at least four semesters**
  before earning a degree, and **transfer candidates may not forfeit any previously earned credit**.
  Because of that, Brown **cannot consider applicants who will have accrued more than four semesters
  of college work (or equivalent) before enrolling.**
- Brown requires a minimum of 28 credits transferred; all eligible college credits must transfer.
- Transfers must complete at least **four semesters and 15 courses at Brown**.
- Students who already hold a bachelor's degree are ineligible.
- **College credit earned while still in high school (dual enrollment / early college) cannot be
  transferred to Brown**, and such students must apply as first-year applicants.
- **Only ONE transfer application per intended calendar year of entry.** Applying in October for
  January entry bars a March application for the following September.
- Brown publishes two entry calendars: **October 1, 2026** for Spring 2027 and **March 1, 2027**
  for Fall 2027. A candidate may submit only one transfer application per calendar year of entry.
- Test-optional for transfer applicants through the 2026-27 cycle.
- Two letters of recommendation from college instructors or teaching assistants.

## 2027 calendar and quarter-system transcript timing

- **Spring 2027:** application due October 1, 2026; mid-term report November 3; final Fall 2026
  transcript due January 6, 2027.
- **Fall 2027:** application due March 1, 2027; quarter-system applicants submit Winter 2027 final
  grades by April 1 and Spring 2027 progress grades by April 16; final 2026–27 transcript due June 15.
- This is direct evidence that Brown may use a UCI spring quarter that begins after the March 1
  application, unlike schools requiring every prerequisite complete before application.

## Majors requested
Brown has an **Open Curriculum** with no distribution requirements and no major-specific admission —
you are admitted to Brown and declare a concentration by the end of the fourth semester.
- **Applied Mathematics, A.B./Sc.B.** (Division of Applied Mathematics)
- **Mathematics, A.B./Sc.B.**
- **Computer Science, A.B./Sc.B.**
- **Electrical Engineering** — offered as the electrical track of Brown's **Engineering Sc.B.**
There are no published per-concentration transfer course prerequisites; coursework matters only as
evidence of preparation.
