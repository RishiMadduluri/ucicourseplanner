# Duke University — transfer research

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

## Admission
Source: https://undergraduate.bulletins.duke.edu/policies-procedures/admissions
- "A limited number of transfer seats may be available, and the number will vary from year to year."
- Duke considers transfer applicants entering as **sophomores or juniors**.
- **Deadline March 15**; decisions mid-May; reply by June 1.
- Apply with the **Coalition Application or the Duke Transfer Application** (not the Common App).
- Required: application forms, official transcripts from all accredited colleges, **high school
  records**, SAT or ACT scores, and employment records if there was extended employment.
  (The Bulletin lists SAT/ACT among required transfer materials while Duke's stated policy is
  **test-optional for first-year and transfer admission** — submit if you have strong scores.)
- Applicants are admitted to either **Trinity College of Arts & Sciences** or the
  **Pratt School of Engineering**.
- **Calculus is a prerequisite for admission to Pratt.** Physics is strongly recommended
  (Pratt applicants are advised to have four years of math and four of science, including physics).
- Successful transfer applicants typically present about a **3.7+ college GPA** in a rigorous program.
  Duke's transfer admit rate has recently been in the ~2% range — tighter than its first-year rate.
- **Duke grants credit for no more than two years of coursework completed elsewhere**, and a transfer
  student must **spend at least two years at Duke**.
- Trinity transfers must satisfy the Trinity curriculum on top of the major; "for most transfer
  students, this will require up to six semesters at Duke."
- Once enrolled, moving between Trinity and Pratt requires a separate internal transfer application
  after one completed semester.

## Majors requested
1. **Computer Science, B.S./B.A.** — Trinity College of Arts & Sciences.
2. **Electrical and Computer Engineering, B.S.E.** — Pratt School of Engineering.
3. **Interdepartmental Major (IDM): Mathematics + Computer Science on Data Science** — Trinity.
4. **Interdepartmental Major (IDM): Statistical Science + Computer Science on Data Science** — Trinity.
   Source: https://cs.duke.edu/undergraduate/degrees/IDM
   - An IDM draws equally on two Trinity departments: **minimum 14 courses, 7 in each department,
     with at least 10 at the 200 level or above.**
   - The Stat+CS pathway suits students interested in the statistical underpinnings of data science;
     the Math+CS pathway covers more of the mathematical foundations.
   - IDMs are declared at Duke; they are not separate admission targets.
5. **Mathematics, B.S./B.A.** — Trinity.

Note: for Duke, only the SCHOOL (Trinity vs Pratt) is an admission target. All four Trinity programs
above are one admission decision; ECE is the separate Pratt decision.
